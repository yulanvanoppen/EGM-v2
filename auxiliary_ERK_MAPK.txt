********** MODEL NAME
ERK_MAPK

********** MODEL NOTES
Markevich et al. (2004) MAPK double phosphorylation/dephosphorylation with
MAPKK (KK, kinase) and MKP3 (P, phosphatase); microscopic (mass-action) model
with BioModels parameter values (BIOMD0000000026). Units: nM, s.
Kinase:      M+KK <-> MKK -> Mp+KK (k1,km1,k2);  Mp+KK <-> MpKK -> Mpp+KK (k3,km3,k4)
Phosphatase: Mpp+P <-> MppP -> MpP (h1,hm1,h2);  MpP <-> Mp+P (h3,hm3)
             Mp+P <-> MpPs -> MP (h4,hm4,h5);    MP <-> M+P (h6,hm6)
States Mpt and Mppt are the measured totals:
  Mpt  = Mp + MpKK + MpP + MpPs   (y1, total single-phosphorylated MAPK)
  Mppt = Mpp + MppP               (y2, total double-phosphorylated MAPK)
so free Mp = Mpt-MpKK-MpP-MpPs and free Mpp = Mppt-MppP.
Enzyme levels reduced (KK(0)=1 instead of 50, P(0)=2 instead of 100) to operate
in the QSSA regime; M(0)=150 instead of 500 for better practical identifiability.
Dynamics are ~50x slower than with the original enzyme levels.
